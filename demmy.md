## Quick Steps:

1. **Copy your files** to `src/routes/`
2. **Add the TanStack Router wrapper** at the top of each file (3 lines)
3. **Rename your default export** function to match the component name in the route
4. **Keep everything else the same** - all your logic, components, styles stay identical
